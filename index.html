A small organic farm in the heart of Oxfordshire

Hampton Gay is a traditional organic farm on the River Cherwell, about 7 miles north of Oxford. Our Old English Longhorn cattle are Pasture for Life certified: they eat nothing but grass and herbs, and live outside in family groups all year round. Our sheep graze the same herb-rich pastures, and we let them grow slowly, which is why our hogget and mutton have such depth of flavour.

We farm with a light touch. The animals largely look after their own health on natural pastures, so we rarely need the vet or antibiotics. The result is meat with real flavour, and we know exactly what's in it.
